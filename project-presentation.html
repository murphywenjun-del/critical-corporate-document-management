The Problem: Fragmented Documents, Real Risk

Where Are the Documents?

  • Scattered across network drives, OneDrive, and third-party vendors
  • Many agreements exist only as paper copies on staff desks
  • Legacy IT contracts are low-quality scans
  • No unified taxonomy or tracking system

What's at Stake?

Contracts signed by unauthorized directors — rendering them legally unenforceable

  • Policy requires two VP signatures for binding documents
  • No automated way to verify signing authority
  • No systematic tracking of compliance gaps
🎙️ "We heard from the CIO that past contracts were signed by people without authority — meaning the organization couldn't legally enforce them. That's the core risk we're solving for."

Core Deliverables 1–4

1

Document Taxonomy & Framework

Standardized metadata schema covering NDAs, MOUs, procurement contracts, policies, board-level agreements. Designed for SharePoint/OneDrive integration.

2

Risk Exposure Assessment

Framework to identify documents that are: missing, expired, not executed per policy, held by individuals instead of the org, or with inadequate indemnities.

3

Risk Category Exploration

Classify risk across four dimensions with SMEs: Operational, Financial, Reputational, and Continuity/Succession — each with specific indicators.

4

Risk Reduction Recommendations

Full cost analysis: system build cost, FTE staffing requirements, operational overhead, and a prioritized roadmap for implementation.

🎙️ "Deliverables 1 and 2 are the foundation — without a clear taxonomy and risk framework, nothing else works. Deliverable 4 is what makes this actionable: we don't just identify problems, we give you the cost and staffing picture to solve them."

Stretch & Administrative Deliverables 5–8

5

AI Review Prototype Stretch

"Tickler" system using de-identified templates to flag: missing fields, expiry dates, renewal triggers, obligation/milestone deadlines, variable financial terms, retention violations, duplicates, and conflicting versions.

6

Governance Policy Stretch

Recommended document governance policy and intake process to prevent future fragmentation — ensuring new documents follow the same standards from day one.

7

Bi-Weekly Status Reports Admin

Written reports every two weeks covering progress, risks/issues with mitigation, planned activities, earned value graphic, and required client decisions.

8

Final Presentation Admin

30–45 minute synchronous presentation to all stakeholders via MS Teams, followed by Q&A and collection of the Client Acceptance Report.

📌 All AI development complies with Southlake's AI governance framework: Microsoft ecosystem, Canadian-hosted models only, DHLC review required, no US cloud infrastructure.

🎙️ "The AI prototype is the most exciting piece — it's essentially a smart tickler system that catches compliance gaps before they become problems. And everything we build follows your AI policy: no US cloud, explainable logic, and full documentation so your team can replicate it."
