```
DATA SHARING AGREEMENT

This Data Sharing Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective
March 3, 2025, between:

SOUTHLAKE HEALTH ("Provider")
AND
PROVINCIAL HEALTH INSTITUTE ("Recipient")

1. PURPOSE AND LIMITATION
1.1 The parties share de-identified clinical datasets for population
health research. The Recipient shall use the data solely for the
stated research purpose and no secondary commercial purpose.

2. PHIPA COMPLIANCE
2.1 The parties shall comply with the Personal Health Information
Protection Act (PHIPA). No re-identification attempts are
permitted, and linkage of datasets requires a new written
authorization from the Provider.

3. AUDIT RIGHTS
3.1 The Provider may audit the Recipient's data-handling controls
annually, with ten (10) business days' notice and reasonable
cooperation by the Recipient.

4. DATA MINIMIZATION
4.1 Datasets shall be limited to the minimum necessary fields.
Direct identifiers are stripped at the source before transfer.

5. RETENTION AND BREACH
5.1 Shared data is retained for five (5) years, then destroyed with
written certification.
5.2 Confirmed or suspected breaches are reported to the Provider
within twenty-four (24) hours.

SIGNATURES

____________________________        ____________________________
Southlake Health, by:             Provincial Health Institute, by:
Hana Osei, VP Research            Victor Lindqvist, VP Data
Date: 2025-03-03                  Date: 2025-03-05
```
