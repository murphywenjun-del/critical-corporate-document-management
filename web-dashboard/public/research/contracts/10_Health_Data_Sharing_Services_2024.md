HEALTH DATA SHARING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Health Data Sharing Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made
effective August 1, 2024, between:

SOUTHLAKE HEALTH ("Provider")
AND
REGIONAL HOSPITAL AUTHORITY ("Recipient")

1. PURPOSE
1.1 The parties share de-identified patient outcomes data to improve
continuity of care across referral pathways.
1.2 The data may be provided to Recipient's designated research
collaborators and auditors who are bound by equivalent
confidentiality obligations.

2. EXPIRY
2.1 This Agreement expires on August 1, 2026, unless extended by
written amendment signed by both parties.

3. PHIPA COMPLIANCE
3.1 The parties shall comply with the Personal Health Information
Protection Act (PHIPA) and the regulations made under it.
3.2 Recipient shall not attempt to re-identify de-identified data.

4. AMENDMENT No. 1 (executed March 15, 2026)
4.1 Amendment No. 1 extends the expiry date of this Agreement and
adds the data categories of "diagnostic imaging results" and
"post-discharge outcomes" to the permitted data elements.
4.2 The renewal window opens 90 days before expiry.

SIGNATURES

Priya Raman, VP Clinical          Elena Brodie, VP Health
Information                      Services, Regional Hospital
Date: 2024-08-01                  Date: 2024-08-02
