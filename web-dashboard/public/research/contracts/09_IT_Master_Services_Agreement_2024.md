```
IT MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective
June 1, 2024, between:

SOUTHLAKE HEALTH
An Academic Family Health Team
1085 Davis Drive, Suite 200
Aurora, Ontario L4G 0B5
("Customer")

AND

CLOUDWORKS INFRASTRUCTURE INC.
2200 Bayview Street
Toronto, Ontario M4G 3A8
("Provider")

1. TERM
1.1 This Agreement commences on June 1, 2024 and continues for a
three (3) year period, expiring May 31, 2027.
1.2 This Agreement auto-renews for successive one (1) year periods
unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least
sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

2. SERVICES
2.1 Provider shall deliver cloud infrastructure hosting, virtualization,
backup, and managed support for Customer's clinical and administrative
systems, including systems that process PHI (protected health
information) for in-patients and out-patients.
2.2 Service levels: Provider shall ensure aggregate platform
availability of 99.9% measured monthly, with service credits of
10% of the monthly fee for each 0.1% of availability shortfall.

3. FEES
3.1 Customer shall pay Provider an annual base fee of $320,000 plus a
support fee of $45,000 per year, payable quarterly in advance.

4. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY
4.1 Provider maintains commercial general liability insurance of
$5,000,000 per occurrence and errors-and-omissions coverage of
$5,000,000.
4.2 Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer
against third-party claims arising from Provider's negligence, and
such indemnity is not subject to a monetary cap.

5. SIGNATURES

Southlake Health, by:
____________________________        ____________________________
Dana Whitfield, VP Information    Marcus Tan, VP Operations
Technology                      Date: 2024-06-01
Date: 2024-06-01
```

**Expected Outcome**: Legal review should add a monetary cap to the 4.2 indemnity, insert a PHI data-security annex, and document the service-credit mechanism against the 99.9% uptime guarantee.
