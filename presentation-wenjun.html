The Problem: Fragmented Documents, Real Risk

Where Are the Documents?

  • Scattered across network drives, OneDrive, and third-party vendors
  • Many agreements exist only as paper copies on staff desks
  • Legacy IT contracts are low-quality scans
  • No unified taxonomy or tracking system

What's at Stake?

Contracts signed by unauthorized directors — rendering them legally unenforceable

  • Policy requires two VP signatures for binding documents
  • No automated way to verify signing authority
  • No systematic tracking of compliance gaps
🎙️ "We heard from the CIO that past contracts were signed by people without authority — meaning the organization couldn't legally enforce them. That's the core risk we're solving for."

Our Three-Phase Approach

Phase 1: Map & Classify

Weeks 1–4
Document taxonomy, inventory & classification framework

Phase 2: Assess & Evaluate

Weeks 5–8
Compliance criteria, risk classification (4 categories)

Phase 3: Build & Recommend

Weeks 9–12
AI prototype, cost analysis & final recommendations

12 weeks total  ·  400 team hours  ·  Bi-weekly status reports  ·  Final synchronous presentation

🎙️ "Three phases over 12 weeks: first we map what you have, then we define what's compliant, and finally we prototype how AI can help you catch risks automatically."
