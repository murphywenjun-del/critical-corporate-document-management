================================================================================
SLIDE 2: The Problem (35 seconds)
================================================================================

"Here's what we heard in our kickoff meeting. Southlake's critical corporate documents like contracts, MOUs, NDAs, policies — are scattered across network drives, and third-party vendors, some still exist only as paper copies on people's desk."

"But the real concern is enforceability. If contracts have been signed by individuals without proper signing authority, those agreements may not be legally binding on the organization."

"The internal policy requires multiple authorized managements to sign any document that binds the corporation. But right now, there's no systematic way to verify that compliance across the entire contract portfolio."

---

================================================================================
SLIDE 3: Our Approach (30 seconds)
================================================================================

"So our approach is structured in three phases over 12 weeks."

"Phase 1 is mapping and classification — we build a standardized document taxonomy so clients know exactly what they have and where it lives. This is already underway."

"Phase 2 is compliance and risk assessment — we define the rules for signing authority verification and classify risks across four dimensions: operational, financial, reputational, and continuity."

"Phase 3 is building and recommending — we prototype an AI-assisted review system and deliver a full cost and staffing analysis for operational implementation."
