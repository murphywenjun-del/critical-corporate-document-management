## 二、周一 Meeting 流程设计（目标：不尴尬、有掌控感）

### 核心原则
- **主动掌控节奏** — 不要被客户带着走，我们要主导 agenda
- **诚实但不虚弱** — 逾期就道歉，没收到 template 就直说，但不要过度解释
- **带着方案去，不是带着问题去** — 每个"不知道"后面跟一个"但我们打算..."
- **让 client 做选择题，不是填空题** — 他们已经够忙了

### 建议流程（60 分钟）

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  0:00-0:05   开场 & Check-in（轻松破冰）                     │
│  0:05-0:15   Phase 1 进展汇报（我们做了什么）                │
│  0:15-0:25   Template + Legal Privilege 跟进                │
│  0:25-0:40   Phase 2 方向讨论（选择题形式）                  │
│  0:40-0:50   Status Report 提交 + 道歉                      │
│  0:50-0:60   确认下次 meeting + 收尾                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 各环节详细脚本

#### 0:00-0:05 开场（1人讲）
> "Hi everyone, thanks for making time. We know it's a busy period — Sam's calendar is packed, so we really appreciate this. We wanted to use this time to update you on Phase 1 progress, get your input on Phase 2 direction, and follow up on the templates and legal privilege. Does that sound good?"

**技巧**：先给 agenda，让客户点头同意。这样他们有了预期，不会觉得被突袭。

---

#### 0:05-0:15 Phase 1 进展汇报（1-2人讲）
**重点**：展示我们已经做了工作，不是空手来的

**Taxonomy 展示**：
> "We've built a preliminary document taxonomy based on what we learned in the kickoff. It covers NDAs, MOUs, procurement contracts, board-level agreements, and policies. We're using a tiered structure — type, department, storage location, and retention requirement. Here's what we have so far..."

**展示内容**（准备 1 页 PPT 或共享屏幕）：
- 当前 taxonomy 草稿（文档类型分类树）
- 标注出"需要 client 确认"的部分

**关键话术**：
> "This is a draft — we want your feedback on whether we're missing any document types or categories. What would you add?"

**技巧**：把 taxonomy 呈现为"半成品邀请客户补充"，而不是"我们什么都没做"。

---

#### 0:15-0:25 Template + Legal Privilege 跟进（1人讲）
**这是最容易尴尬的环节，处理关键**：

**关于 Template**：
> "On the de-identified templates — we sent an email last week following up. We haven't received anything yet, so we wanted to check if there was a delivery issue. Could you confirm how/when they were sent?"

**如果不回复或说"已经发了"**：
> "We'll double-check our spam folder and reach out again after this call. In the meantime, is there a specific format or sample you could share via Teams or OneDrive?"

**关于 Legal Privilege**：
> "We also wanted to check on Tonya's return date and the status of the legal privilege application. This affects when we can move from template-based work to real document analysis."

**如果不方便回答**：
> "No problem, we can follow up separately. For now, we'll continue with the de-identified approach."

**技巧**：不要追问，问了就停。给对方空间，不要显得急躁。

---

#### 0:25-0:40 Phase 2 方向讨论（全员参与）
**用选择题形式，不要开放式提问**：

**问题 1：风险优先级**
> "For Phase 2 risk assessment, we need to prioritize. Which of these is most urgent for you right now?"
> - A) Signing authority compliance (two VP signature verification)
> - B) Document expiration and renewal tracking
> - C) Contract retention and disposal compliance
> - D) All equally important

**问题 2：AI 方案选择**
> "For the AI prototype, we're considering two approaches. Which aligns better with your environment?"
> - A) Microsoft Power Automate + Copilot (faster, uses existing licensing, simplified DHLC pathway)
> - B) Custom Python-based solution with local models (more flexible, longer development, requires full DHLC review)
> - C) We're open to other suggestions

**问题 3：Phase 2 交付物重点**
> "In Phase 2, we can either go deep on one area or broad across multiple. What's more valuable?"
> - A) Deep dive on signing authority compliance only
> - B) Broad risk assessment across all four categories (operational, financial, reputational, continuity)
> - C) Mix: start broad, then focus based on findings

**技巧**：选选择题，让客户说"A"或"B"，不要让他们写论文。

---

#### 0:40-0:50 Status Report 提交 + 道歉（1人讲）
**这是必须面对的，越早越好**：

> "I want to address the status report. It was due September 19th, and I apologize for the delay. We had intended to submit it after the kickoff, but we got caught up in the SOW finalization and template waiting. The first report is attached now. Going forward, we'll maintain the bi-weekly cadence as agreed."

**然后立刻转入**：
> "With that out of the way, let me share what's in it..."

**技巧**：道歉要短、诚恳、不找借口。说完立刻向前看。

---

#### 0:50-0:60 确认下次 meeting + 收尾（1人讲）
> "Before we wrap up, I want to confirm a few things:
> 1. When would be a good time for our next meeting? We know Sam's calendar is tight in late September.
> 2. Can we schedule 1-on-1s with Sonia and Sam for Phase 2 deep dives?
> 3. Is there anything else you need from us before then?
>
> Thanks again for your time. We're making good progress and excited about what we'll build."

**技巧**：用数字列表收尾，让人记得住。

## 五、应急方案（应对尴尬场景）

### 场景 1：Client 问"你们做了什么？"
**应对**：
> "Great question. We've completed the kickoff alignment, signed the SOW, reviewed your AI policy in detail, and drafted a preliminary document taxonomy. We've also prepared the status report that was due last week — I apologize for the delay on that. The taxonomy draft is something we'd love your feedback on."

### 场景 2：Client 说"我还没看到你们的 status report"
**应对**：
> "You're right, and I apologize. It should have been submitted September 19th. I'm sharing it now — [send it]. We'll make sure the bi-weekly cadence is maintained going forward."

### 场景 3：Client 说"template 我已经发了"
**应对**：
> "Thanks for checking. We've searched our inboxes and spam folders and haven't found it. Could you forward it again, or share via OneDrive/Teams? We want to make sure we're not missing anything on our end."

### 场景 4：Client 说" Tonya 还在休假，不知道什么时候回来"
**应对**：
> "Understood. In that case, we'll continue with the de-identified approach and check back next week. We can also schedule the legal privilege discussion for when Tonya is back."

### 场景 5：冷场 / 没人说话
**应对**（主讲人）：
> "Let me rephrase that — [rephrase the question simpler]. Or maybe I should ask a more specific question: [ask a closed yes/no question]."

### 场景 6：Client 给了模糊回答
**应对**：
> "Thanks, that's helpful. Just to make sure I understand correctly — are you saying [repeat back]? And would you say the priority is [A] or [B]?"
