## 二、确认项目理解（0:07 - 0:12）

### 🇬🇧 English

> "Based on the project description we received, here's how we understand the challenge:
>
> Southlake Health currently doesn't have a centralized repository for its critical corporate documents, and there's no systematic framework for assessing the risks associated with those documents. This creates potential exposure in fulfilling legal and contractual obligations.
>
> Our goal is to help you build two capabilities: first, a system to store and manage corporate documents along with the regulations that apply to them; and second, an automated way to detect and notify you of potential risks.
>
> Does that alignment sound right? Are there any aspects we're missing or misunderstanding?"

### 🇨🇳 中文

> 「根据我们收到的项目描述，我们的理解是：
>
> Southlake Health 目前缺少一个统一的关键企业文档存储库，也缺乏系统的风险评估框架，这在履行法律和合同义务时存在潜在风险暴露。
>
> 我们的目标是帮您建立两种能力：第一，一个存储和管理企业文档及相关法律法规的系统；第二，自动检测和通知潜在风险的能力。
>
> 这样理解对吗？有没有我们遗漏或理解偏差的地方？」

---

## 三、需求澄清 — 核心问题（0:12 - 0:45）

### 问题 1：文档类型和范围

#### 🇬🇧 English

> "Let's start with the documents themselves. Can you walk us through what types of 'critical corporate documents' Southlake currently has? For example:
>
> - Are these mostly **contracts and agreements** (vendor contracts, service agreements, partnerships)?
> - **Internal policies and procedures** (HR policies, operational SOPs, compliance frameworks)?
> - **Regulatory and legal documents** (PHIPA compliance docs, FHT agreement terms, OHA requirements)?
> - **Financial documents** (grants, funding agreements, audit reports)?
> - Or a mix of all of the above?
>
> Also, roughly how many documents are we talking about? Is this in the dozens, hundreds, or thousands?"

#### 🇨🇳 中文

> 「先从文档本身开始。能请您介绍一下 Southlake 目前有哪些类型的「关键企业文档」吗？比如：
>
> - 主要是**合同和协议**（供应商合同、服务协议、合作协定）？
> - **内部政策和流程**（人力资源政策、操作 SOP、合规框架）？
> - **法规和法律文件**（PHIPA 合规文件、FHT 协议条款、OHA 要求）？
> - **财务文件**（拨款、资助协议、审计报告）？
> - 还是以上都有？
>
> 另外，大概有多少份文档？几十份、几百份、还是上千份？」

---

### 问题 2：当前文档管理方式

#### 🇬🇧 English

> "Where are these documents currently stored? For example:
>
> - A **shared network drive or SharePoint**?
> - **Email attachments** and individual folders on personal computers?
> - **Physical paper files** in filing cabinets?
> - Some **combination** of the above?
>
> And when someone needs to find a specific document — say, a contract that's about to expire — what does that process look like today? Who handles it, and how much time does it typically take?"

#### 🇨🇳 中文

> 「这些文档目前存放在哪里？比如：
>
> - **共享网络驱动器或 SharePoint**？
> - **邮件附件**和个人电脑上的文件夹？
> - **纸质档案**，放在文件柜里？
> - 以上几种的**混合**？
>
> 另外，当有人需要找一份特定文档——比如一份即将到期的合同——现在的流程是什么样的？谁负责找，通常需要多长时间？」

---

### 问题 3：风险场景 — 最重要

#### 🇬🇧 English

> "This is the most important question for us. You mentioned 'risk exposure within legal and contractual obligations.' Can you give us specific examples of risks that have occurred or are most concerning to you? For instance:
>
> - **Contract expiration risks** — a vendor contract expires without renewal, disrupting service?
> - **Regulatory change risks** — a new regulation comes into effect but your policies haven't been updated to comply?
> - **Compliance gap risks** — a document is required by regulation but isn't actually in place or is outdated?
> - **Obligation tracking risks** — missing a deadline for submitting a report or fulfilling a contractual requirement?
> - **Other risks** we haven't mentioned?
>
> For each type of risk, what's the current process for detecting it? And when problems do occur, what's the impact — is it financial, reputational, operational, or a combination?"

#### 🇨🇳 中文

> 「这是最重要的问题。您提到「法律和合同义务方面的风险暴露」，能请您给我们一些具体的例子吗？比如：
>
> - **合同过期风险** — 供应商合同到期未续签，导致服务中断？
> - **法规变更风险** — 新法规生效但内部政策尚未更新以符合合规要求？
> - **合规缺口风险** — 法规要求有一份文档但实际上没有或已过期？
> - **义务跟踪风险** — 错过了提交报告或履行合同义务的截止日期？
> - **其他我们没有提到的风险**？
>
> 对于每种风险类型，目前是如何发现的？如果出问题，影响是什么——财务上的、声誉上的、运营上的，还是综合性的？」

---

### 问题 4：系统用户和使用场景

#### 🇬🇧 English

> "Who in the organization would be the primary users of this system? For example:
>
> - **Administrative staff** who manage day-to-day document operations?
> - **Legal/compliance team** who need to track regulatory obligations?
> - **Senior management** who need oversight and reporting?
> - **Care Manager team** (from the other Southlake AFP project) who also need access?
>
> And in what scenarios would they interact with the system most? For example: uploading a new contract, searching for a policy, receiving a risk notification, generating a compliance report, etc."

#### 🇨🇳 中文

> 「组织内哪些人是这个系统的主要用户？比如：
>
> - **行政人员**，负责日常文档操作？
> - **法务/合规团队**，需要跟踪法规义务？
> - **高层管理者**，需要监督和报告？
> - **Care Manager 团队**（Southlake 另一个 AFP 项目）也需要访问？
>
> 他们最会在哪些场景下使用系统？比如：上传新合同、搜索政策、接收风险通知、生成合规报告等。」

---

For example, should Care Managers also have access to view certain documents or risk notifications through their system?"

> - **Who should we send our bi-weekly status reports to?** Is there a specific format Ivey requires that we should follow?
> - **For the final presentation**, do you prefer MS Teams or an in-person meeting? How long would you like it to be?
> - **Who is our main point of contact** going forward? And what's the best way to reach them (email, Teams, phone)?"

> 「几个实用的问题，帮助我们做好规划：
>
> - 这个项目的学生团队有**多少人**？
> - 预期的**开始和结束日期**是什么时候？是否有需要配合的固定节点（比如课程截止日期或展示日期）？
> - **双周进度报告**应该发给谁？Ivey 是否有要求的格式？
> - **最终成果展示**，您更倾向于 MS Teams 还是线下？希望多久？
> - **日常联系人**是谁？通过什么方式联系最方便（邮件、Teams、电话）？」
