# HEALTH DATA SHARING & SERVICES AGREEMENT

[BASE AGREEMENT]

This Health Data Sharing & Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made
effective as of September 1, 2024, between:

SOUTHLAKE HEALTH
An Academic Family Health Team
1085 Davis Drive, Suite 200
Aurora, Ontario L4G 0B5
("Disclosing Party")

AND

REGIONAL HOSPITAL AUTHORITY
300 Hospital Boulevard
Newmarket, Ontario L3Y 5Z2
("Receiving Party")

## 1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this Agreement is to facilitate the sharing of
de-identified patient outcome data between the Parties for
quality improvement, research, and care coordination in
community-based health services.

## 2. DATA TO BE SHARED

The following categories of de-identified data shall be shared:
a) Patient demographics (age group, gender, postal code FSA)
b) Treatment outcomes and medication adherence rates
c) 30-day readmission rates
d) Preventive screening completion rates
e) Emergency department utilization metrics

## 3. USE OF DATA

The Receiving Party may use the data solely for:
a) Quality improvement reporting
b) Program evaluation
c) De-identified research analysis

## 4. PHIPA COMPLIANCE

Both Parties agree to comply with the Personal Health Information
Protection Act, 2004, S.O. 2004, c. 3, Sch. 3 ("PHIPA"),
including:
a) Collecting, using, and disclosing personal health information
   only for permitted purposes
b) Implementing appropriate administrative, technical, and
   organizational security safeguards
c) Ensuring all shared data is fully de-identified prior to
   transfer

## 5. DURATION

This Agreement shall remain in effect for two (2) years from
the Effective Date, expiring on August 1, 2026, unless
terminated earlier by either Party with sixty (60) days written
notice.

## 6. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Province
of Ontario and the applicable laws of Canada.

[AMENDMENT NO. 1]

AMENDMENT TO HEALTH DATA SHARING & SERVICES AGREEMENT
dated August 15, 2026

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") amends the Health Data Sharing
& Services Agreement dated September 1, 2024 (the "Agreement")
between SOUTHLAKE HEALTH and REGIONAL HOSPITAL AUTHORITY.

1. TERM EXTENSION
   The expiration date in Section 5 of the Agreement is extended
   from August 1, 2026 to January 1, 2028.

2. ADDITIONAL DATA CATEGORIES
   The following data categories are added to Section 2:
   a) ICD-10 diagnosis codes (de-identified)
   b) Length of stay for hospital admissions
   c) Patient satisfaction survey results (de-identified)

3. EXPANDED USE
   The data use provisions in Section 3 are amended to add:
   d) Sharing with research collaborators (de-identified only,
      subject to separate data use agreements)

4. DATA MINIMIZATION
   Both Parties agree to limit data sharing to the minimum
   necessary for the stated purposes, in accordance with the
   data minimization principle.

5. NO OTHER CHANGES
   All other provisions of the Agreement remain unchanged and in full
   force and effect.
