# IT MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This IT Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective
as of June 1, 2024, between:

SOUTHLAKE HEALTH
An Academic Family Health Team
1085 Davis Drive, Suite 200
Aurora, Ontario L4G 0B5
("Purchaser")

AND

CLOUDWORKS INFRASTRUCTURE INC.
2200 Technology Parkway, Suite 400
Mississauga, Ontario L5B 1Z4
("Vendor")

## 1. SERVICES

Vendor shall provide the following services to Purchaser:
a) Cloud infrastructure hosting for electronic health record (EHR) systems
b) 24/7/365 monitoring and incident response
c) Quarterly performance reviews and capacity planning
d) Disaster recovery and business continuity support
e) Ongoing patch management and security updates

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

This Agreement shall commence on June 1, 2024 and continue for a
period of three (3) years, expiring on May 31, 2027.

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year
periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal
at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

a) Base infrastructure fee: $320,000/year (billed monthly at $26,667)
b) Support services: $45,000/year (billed monthly at $3,750)
c) Overage fees: $0.05 per additional GB of data transfer beyond 10TB
d) Fees are subject to annual increase of three percent (3%) or CPI,
   whichever is greater, capped at 5% per annum
e) Payment terms: Net thirty (30) days from invoice date
f) All fees are in Canadian dollars (CAD)

## 4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

a) Uptime guarantee: 99.9% during business hours (6:00 AM – 10:00 PM,
   Monday through Saturday)
b) For downtime exceeding the guaranteed level, Vendor shall provide
   service credits equal to five percent (5%) of monthly fees per
   hour of unplanned downtime
c) Response times:
   - Priority 1 (system down): 2 hours
   - Priority 2 (degraded service): 4 hours
   - Priority 3 (minor issue): 8 hours
d) Vendor shall provide monthly SLA performance reports
e) Three (3) consecutive months of SLA failure entitles Purchaser
   to terminate this Agreement without penalty

## 5. DATA SECURITY AND PRIVACY

a) Vendor shall maintain commercially reasonable physical, technical,
   and administrative safeguards to protect data in transit and at rest
b) All data shall be encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit
c) Vendor acknowledges it may have access to de-identified operational
   data related to EHR system performance and capacity metrics
d) In the event of a security incident affecting data, Vendor shall
   notify Purchaser within forty-eight (48) hours

## 6. INDEMNIFICATION

a) Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Purchaser from
   and against any claims, damages, and losses arising from Vendor's
   negligence, breach, or failure to perform its obligations
b) Vendor's indemnification obligations shall not be subject to
   a monetary cap
c) Purchaser shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Purchaser's
   use of the Services in violation of applicable law

## 7. INSURANCE

Vendor shall maintain:
a) Commercial General Liability: $2,000,000 per occurrence
b) Cyber Liability / Technology E&O: $1,000,000 per claim
c) Professional Liability: $1,000,000 per occurrence
d) Evidence of insurance to be provided upon request

## 8. TERMINATION

a) Either Party may terminate for material breach with sixty (60) days
   written notice and failure to cure
b) Purchaser may terminate for convenience with ninety (90) days written
   notice
c) Upon termination, Vendor shall:
   i) Provide a transition period of not less than thirty (30) days
   ii) Return or destroy all Purchaser data within thirty (30) days
   iii) Provide certification of data destruction
d) Termination shall not relieve either Party of obligations accrued
   prior to the effective date of termination

## 9. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario
and the applicable laws of Canada.
