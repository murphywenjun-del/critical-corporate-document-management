## Amendment Text

```
AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DATA SHARING AGREEMENT
(document status: PENDING EXECUTION)

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") amends the Data Sharing
Agreement dated January 9, 2025 (the "Agreement") between
Southlake Health and the Provider Data Exchange.

1. RETENTION PERIOD
1.1 The retention period for shared datasets is extended from
three (3) years to five (5) years from the date of each data
transfer, in accordance with Schedule A R12.

2. CLASSIFICATION CODES
2.1 All diagnostic fields shall be mapped to ICD-10-CM codes at
the time of extraction; legacy ICD-9 crosswalks are retired as
of the effective date of this Amendment.

3. BREACH NOTIFICATION
3.1 The breach notification window is strengthened from forty-eight
(48) hours to twenty-four (24) hours from the time the receiving
party becomes aware of a confirmed or suspected breach.

4. SECURITY TESTING
4.1 The Provider shall complete annual penetration testing of the
exchange environment and deliver a summary report to Southlake
Health within thirty (30) days of completion.

5. EXECUTION
5.1 This Amendment takes effect when signed by both parties.
```
